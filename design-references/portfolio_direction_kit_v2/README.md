# Portfolio Direction Kit — v2

This folder is the **single source of truth** for the portfolio site.

This version updates the earlier brief with one major refinement:

> The site should still be minimal and surface-driven, but it should carry **more of the colors of sunrise/daybreak**, and the **animation must reflect that**.

The portfolio should therefore feel like:

- a calm neutral surface
- gradually touched by dawn
- with subtle but perceptible color richness
- expressed through light, shadow, material, typography, and restrained motion

The site should **not** become a literal sunrise illustration.

Instead, it should present:

> **a minimalist portfolio whose atmosphere evolves through the chromatic effects of daybreak.**

A second design idea remains:

> Scrolling should feel very gently like moving across a tiny visible portion of an enormous curved surface, as though the page is revolving beneath the viewer.

This globe-like effect must remain secondary and nearly imperceptible.

## Core creative sentence

> Design a minimal portfolio as a quiet neutral surface that gradually fills with the colors and effects of daybreak through subtle light, shadow, material texture, typography, and restrained motion, with a Japanese-influenced sensibility rooted in emptiness, precision, atmosphere, and restraint.

## Core motion sentence

> Animation should make the page feel like morning is arriving: light slowly shifting, shadows drifting, tones warming, and muted sunrise hues gently appearing across the surface while the page remains elegant, legible, and calm.

## Non-negotiable restraint

If a color or motion effect becomes loud, obvious, or decorative, it is too strong.

Desired reaction:

> “This feels like dawn, but in a refined, understated way.”

Not:

> “This is a sunrise animation.”
