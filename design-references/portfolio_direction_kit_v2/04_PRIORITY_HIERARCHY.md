# 04 — Priority Hierarchy

This file is authoritative.

When tradeoffs arise, higher priorities win.

## Priority 1 — Typography and composition

Protect:
- readability
- hierarchy
- line length
- spacing
- alignment
- rhythm
- content clarity

If any color or motion effect harms these, reduce or remove the effect.

---

## Priority 2 — Portfolio content and usability

Protect:
- clear understanding of the work
- navigation
- discoverability
- accessibility
- responsive clarity
- semantic structure

The site exists to present the portfolio, not the atmosphere.

---

## Priority 3 — Light, shadow, and dawn-color atmosphere

This is the primary expressive layer.

Prioritize:
- believable light direction
- quiet shadow geometry
- subtle dawn-color progression
- calm atmospheric continuity

This is more important than the globe effect.

---

## Priority 4 — Materiality

Subtle surface richness should keep the site from feeling sterile.

Materiality supports the light/color system.

---

## Priority 5 — Motion

Motion should support atmosphere and continuity.

If the static page is weak, motion will not save it.

---

## Priority 6 — Globe-like spatial sensation

This remains optional flavor.

If it:
- hurts readability
- becomes noticeable
- complicates responsiveness
- harms performance
- competes with the dawn atmosphere

then weaken or remove it.

---

## Priority 7 — Technical novelty

Do not prefer technical complexity for its own sake.

Do not use WebGL, Three.js, or advanced animation infrastructure unless clearly justified.

---

# Final tradeoff rule

When in doubt:

> Prefer the quieter, clearer, more legible, more believable, and more atmospheric version.
